# Kingdom of Ashes — World & Art roadmap

Direction: original 2.5D isometric medieval visuals, inspired by classic Age of Empires II architecture and Diablo II atmosphere. Keep existing save, combat and streamed scenery.

| Version | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 15.8 | Landscape artwork: 8 terrain types, 4 tree types, 5 detail types, shores and cached regional textures | Implemented; Chromium validation pending |
| 15.9 | Distinct city architecture, castle, buildings, interiors | Planned |
| 16.0 | Character and equipment sprites, eight-direction movement and attacks | Planned |
| 16.1 | 30 distinct creatures, loot and level bands | Planned |
| 16.2 | 256×256 continent, Blackwood, swamp, Frostgard, Akhar, Ignareth, Morghul, Stormhaven, Nharoth in stages | Planned |
| 16.3 | Creature aggression, fleeing, pack behavior, ranged combat, poison, elemental resistances | Planned |
| 16.4 | Caves, dungeons and distinct bosses | Planned |
| 16.5 | 384×384 world | Planned |
| 16.6 | 512×512 world | Planned |
| 17.0 | Consolidation and balance | Planned |

15.8 is procedural raster artwork, not final hand-painted production sprites. No external copyrighted game assets. Preserve stable root version.
